## Phase Overview

```
Phase 0 — Arrival          God summons you, character creation, King, first companion, first city
Phase 1 — Growth            Level up, recruit, do missions, meet other Godsents
Phase 1.5 — Betrayal        King sends you + another party to Shadow World. They betray you.
Phase 2 — Shadow World      Trapped underground. Dangerous. Underground cities. Can't return.
Phase 2.5 — Emergence       Escape Shadow World. Return to people who thought you were dead.
Phase 3 — Revenge           Find traitors (Phase 3 boss). Build gear at blacksmith. Help kingdom.
Phase 4 — Final Battle      Journey to final boss. Mini-stories along the way.
Phase 4.5 — Epilogue        Ending based on world tags from your choices.
```

## Phase 0 — Arrival (Detailed)

### Sequence:
1. **God summons you** — isekai moment, tells you you're crossing worlds
2. **Character creation** — avatar, class, stats, world skill, class passive choice
3. **Summoning circle** — you appear in the new world above a summoning circle
4. **Other Godsents** — you look around and see many other summoned heroes
5. **The King speaks** — explains the world, why he summoned heroes, what's at stake
6. **Equipment & gold** — King gives minimal equipment based on class + starting gold
7. **Companion selection** — choose 1 of 3 companions
8. **Departure** — you leave in pairs to the first city
9. **Bar scene** — companion tells you their story, gives advice on what to do
10. **City access** — enter shops, prepare
11. **First mission** — choose from 3 mission options, set out

### Notes:
- The gods summoned MANY heroes, not just you. Each Godsent gets some equipment and one hero from the kingdom as a partner.
- You'll meet other Godsent groups throughout the game on missions.
- There is always a SHOP button when in the overworld (costs time to visit city).

## Phase 1 — Growth

- Level up, earn gold and items
- Recruit party members (beyond initial companion)
- Do missions at varying difficulties (choose from 3 each time)
- Meet other Godsent groups on some missions
- Companion stories begin running
- Cross-phase story seeds planted (help someone → they reappear later)
- **Ends with: First Boss** (selected from a pool of possible bosses)

---

## Phase 1.5 — Shadow World Entry (Betrayal)

- Triggered: immediately after Phase 1 boss
- The King sends you AND another Godsent party (the two best parties) to investigate something in the Shadow World
- You travel together with the other party
- **The other party BETRAYS you** — they switched sides, they want to eliminate competition
- They trap you in the Shadow World
- The traitor party changes every playthrough (see Traitor System below)

---

## Phase 2 — The Shadow World

- Very dangerous, lots of opportunities
- **Cannot return to main city**
- Occasionally reach underground cities (shop available there)
- Occasionally meet merchants (shop available)
- The Shadow World is described as: a place known for many dangers and opportunities, dark mages go there, bad things come out of it occasionally
- **Possible stories:**
  - Meet another Godsent group stuck down here
  - Find corpses of a Godsent group you met earlier — loot them
  - Solo separation story (trap/test splits you from party)
  - Arena in underground city
  - Cultivation/training arc in underground area
  - Memory monster (fight it, your memory gets hit, story starts mid-way with timeline jumps)
- **Ends with: Phase 2 Boss** (selected from pool)

---

## Phase 2.5 — Emergence

- Against all odds, you escape the Shadow World
- Return to main city
- Talk to people who thought you were dead
- World has changed — see impact of the invasion/threat on the kingdom
- Transition to revenge arc

---

## Phase 3 — Revenge & Preparation

- Full city access again
- **Blacksmith:** Can build things from rare/cool items found in the Shadow World
- **Mage:** Crafting materials found below unlock options
- Find the traitor party → **they are the Phase 3 boss**
- Traitors now have powerful items given to them by the final boss
- OR: journey toward the final boss location
- Continue getting stronger, helping people
- See the consequences of all your earlier choices
- Cross-phase stories pay off (people you helped return, enemies you spared reappear)
- **Ends with: Traitor Party Boss Fight**

---

## Phase 4 — Final Confrontation

- Mini-stories during the journey/battle against the final boss
- Could be:
  - Climbing the Necromancer's tower
  - Entering the Demon Realm to defeat the Demon Lord
  - Ascending the Dragon's fortress
- **Final boss: NOT decided yet** (always the same boss, unlike Phases 1-3)
- **Ends with: Final Boss Fight**

---

## Phase 4.5 — Epilogue

- Ending determined by **world tags** accumulated from all your choices
- Different endings based on: who you helped, who you spared, companions alive, items found, etc.

## Boss System

| Phase | Boss Selection | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Phase 1 | **Pool of bosses** — randomly selected | Different each playthrough |
| Phase 2 | **Pool of bosses** — randomly selected | Different each playthrough |
| Phase 3 | **Always the traitor party** | Varies because traitor party varies |
| Phase 4 | **Always the same final boss** | Fixed — the "big bad" |

Each phase has a **start story** and an **end story** (the boss).

---

## Traitor Party System

The traitor party is a **roguelite variable** — different every playthrough:

| Phase | Traitor Interaction |
|-------|-------------------|
| Phase 1 | You just SEE them (introduction, seems friendly) |
| Phase 1.5 | They betray you and trap you in the Shadow World |
| Phase 2 | You're stuck because of them |
| Phase 3 | They are the BOSS — now equipped with powerful items from the final boss |

- The traitor party type changes based on which boss is assigned to Phase 3
- Build the Phase 3 boss fight to reflect the traitors' equipment and abilities
- Different party compositions each playthrough (warrior-heavy, mage-heavy, etc.)

## Cross-Phase Stories

Stories that span multiple phases, creating narrative payoff:

| Seed (Early Phase) | Payoff (Later Phase) | Example |
|--------------------|---------------------|---------|
| Help someone in Phase 1 | They appear in Shadow World | The merchant you saved runs a shop underground |
| Barely escape an enemy in Phase 1 | Beat them after Shadow World | Revenge encounter in Phase 3 |
| Show mercy to a demon | They return in Phase 4 | Help you... or betray you |
| Meet a Godsent group in Phase 1 | Find their corpses in Shadow World | Loot their gear, feel the loss |
| Meet a stuck group in Shadow World | They escape and help in Phase 4 | Allied reinforcements |

These are tracked through the **tag system**.

---

## Other Godsent Groups

Throughout the game, you encounter other summoned hero groups:

- Groups you help → come back to help you later
- A strong group you meet in the final part
- A group that died mid-mission
- A dead group whose corpses you find in the Shadow World (loot them)
- A group stuck in the Shadow World you meet in an underground city
- They activate certain **world tags** if you do missions with them
- Some are **multi-part mission chains**

---

## Companion Stories

- Run across **all phases** — long narrative arcs
- Over time companions get: items specific to them, special abilities, stronger
- **Recruitment:** Short acquaintance story when joining, learn about them through gameplay
- **In mission selection:** Companion stories appear as one of the 3 options
  - Companion's name + portrait shown next to description
  - Only 1 companion story per selection round
  - **If you skip it, you miss it permanently** (story is gone)

---

## Mission Selection System

Before each mission departure:
1. Player sees **3 mission options** with short descriptions
2. Each has a different difficulty (indicated by time cost)
3. One might be a companion story (marked with portrait)
4. Player chooses one
5. The unchosen missions may or may not be available next time

---

## Story Setup at New Game

When starting a new game:
1. **Fixed stories** are placed: all bosses, King intro, companion selection pool
2. **Random stories** fill remaining slots from the story pool
3. **Continuation priority:** When you complete a multi-part story, its sequels immediately take random slots (kicking out other random stories)
4. **Failure handling:** If you fail a mission and it has no continuation path, the planned sequels disappear and random stories replace them
