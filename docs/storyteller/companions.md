## Companion Story Arcs (Detailed)

### #4 — Mage #1 (Elf Male, Act 0)

**Concept:** Apprentice of a Grand Wizard. Elf raised by humans — grew up in the wizard's tower since childhood. Currently works for the King. Wants to join the Godsent's quest.

**Recruitment:** Available in Act 0 at the King's Guild. Eager to prove himself, wants adventure connected to his master's research.

**Story Arc (multi-act chain):**
1. **Early quests:** Tasks connected to his master — fetch reagents, investigate magical anomalies, deliver reports. Seems like normal apprentice duties.
2. **Mid quests:** Discovers disturbing things about his master's past apprentices — they all "graduated" and disappeared. Grows stronger through training.
3. **Late quests:** His master attempts to possess his body. The Grand Wizard has been doing this for centuries — taking over his apprentices' bodies to extend his life. He specifically kidnapped this elf as a child because elves are ideal vessels for possession (long-lived, magically receptive).
4. **Climax:** Boss fight against the Grand Wizard (who may be in a previous apprentice's body or his true form). Save the Mage or lose him.

**Hidden Truth:** The Mage never knew he was kidnapped. He genuinely believes the wizard raised him out of kindness. The revelation that his entire life was grooming for possession is the emotional core.

**Why Elf raised by humans:** The wizard needed an elf specifically — eligibility for possession. Took him from an elven settlement as an infant. The Mage's "human upbringing" isn't a cultural quirk, it's evidence of the crime.

### #6 — Warrior #2 "One-Armed Warrior" (Orc Male, Act 1)

**Concept:** Orc who was bullied his whole life for being an orc. Joined a party, they got wiped fighting Gorath's forces. Lost his arm in the same battle. Now broken, homeless, ready to give up.

**Recruitment:** Found at his lowest in Act 1. You invite him to fight one last time — with you. He accepts not because he believes in himself, but because someone finally believed in him.

**DOUBLE HANDICAP (gameplay cost):**
- **Swimming (world skill):** DEAD. Can't swim with one arm. Picking him = running with effectively 3 world skills instead of 4.
- **2H Weapons:** Can't use them. Has maxed 2H investment but physically cannot wield. Locked to 1H only.
- **Result:** Genuinely the weakest companion at recruitment. Real tradeoff — you sacrifice exploration utility for a long-term combat investment.

**Story Arc (multi-act chain):**
1. **Phase 1 — Broken:** Depressed, one-armed, barely functional. Locked to 1H weapons. Dead world skill. Noticeably less damage than other frontliners (Gina with her big axe outperforms him).
2. **Phase 2 — Fighting again:** Masters one-arm fighting. Earns respect. Hints about a dwarven forge.
3. **Phase 3 — Dwarven Fortress:** Journey to forge an iron prosthetic arm. Quest chain at dwarven mountain fortress.
4. **Phase 4 — Reborn:** Prosthetic complete. Unlocks Swimming + 2H weapon equip. Becomes one of the strongest companions overnight — his 2H skills were already maxed. Massive power spike.
5. **Secret evolution:** Three-handed weapon (ultimate form). Details TBD.

**Character Arc:** Starts depressed and quiet. Over time becomes your ride-or-die bro. The heart of the party. The player who sticks with him through his weak phase gets massively rewarded.

**Unique item:** Iron Prosthetic Arm (companion equipment, sets tag `warrior_has_prosthetic`)

## Design Notes
- Each companion has 1 world skill
- Every companion has a personal quest chain (recruitment → bonding → personal quest → resolution)
- Recruitment quests appear during missions until party full
- First companion: choose 1 of 3 from King's Guild or refuse all
- Skip a companion's recruitment = miss them permanently
- Companion quest success = upgrade/unlock. Failure = stays same (no death from quest failure)
- Party = 2 front + 2 back (4 total including player)
- Act 0 offers 3 front + 2 back. Act 1 adds 2 front + 3 back + 1 flex. Act 2 adds 1 front + 1 back.

## Combat Skill Notes

- **Bard active: Song of Speed** — Boosts whole party's ATB speed. Fills 1 of 2 missing Bard actives.
