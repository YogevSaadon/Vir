# Vassnian Lore Bible (Canonical)

All decisions locked. This is the canonical reference for all story writing.

---

## The Kingdom of Caelmund

- **People:** Caelmundi
- **Government:** Feudal kingdom ruled by a King
- **History:** Founded after the Sealing War that imprisoned Gorath. Centuries of peace made it strong but complacent.

### The First City (Hub)
- **The Citadel** — King's seat, military command
- **The Market Quarter** — shops, blacksmith, apothecary, taverns
- **The Circle of Arrival** — where Godsents appear (ancient stone summoning platform)
- **The Temple District** — churches to Auron, Maetha, Luminar
- **The Lower Quarter** — common folk, cheaper lodging, information brokers

### Surface Races

| Race | Status |
|------|--------|
| **Humans** | Dominant, rule the kingdom |
| **Elves** | Forest enclaves, reclusive, ancient |
| **Dwarves** | Mountain fortresses, master forgers |
| **Orcs** | Former enemies, grudging allies, uneasy peace |
| **Halflings/Gnomes** | Merchants, tinkers, innkeepers, information brokers |
| **Beastfolk** | Tribal clans (wolf, cat, bird, lizard, etc.) |
| **Undead/Cursed** | NOT a race — a condition tied to Gorath's corruption |

**Race relations:** Cities are mixed/cosmopolitan. Rural areas are single-race and insular.

## The Pantheon

| God | Domain | Worshipped By |
|-----|--------|---------------|
| **Auron** | War | Soldiers, knights, paladins, warriors |
| **Maetha** | Life | Healers, farmers, common folk, priests |
| **Luminar** | Knowledge | Mages, scholars, seekers of truth |
| **Gorath** | Conquest, Corruption, Domination | Cultists, the desperate (SEALED) |
| **Umbrath** | Shadow, Secrets | Forgotten on surface; worshipped in Shadow World |

### Divine Rules
- Gods NEVER appear directly to mortals (signs, dreams, visions, priests only)
- Player never meets any god face-to-face
- Priest class passive (Divine Favor): pick deity at character creation for deity-specific story choices

### The Summoning Mystery
A god secretly rigged the Rite of Calling. **Who is deliberately ambiguous.** All five are suspects:
- Auron (protecting world through a chosen warrior)
- Maetha (preserving life by selecting the right soul)
- Luminar (calculated the optimal hero)
- Umbrath (working from shadows for unknown reasons)
- Gorath (wanting a worthy opponent or unwitting pawn)

---

## Gorath / The Dark God

- **Nature:** God of conquest, corruption, domination. Wants to RULE, not destroy. Corrupts rather than annihilates.
- **Origin:** Waged war thousands of years ago. Coalition of mortals + divine intervention SEALED him.
- **The Seal:** Anchored to sacred sites. After millennia, binding magic is eroding.

### Seal Progression by Act

| Act | Signs |
|-----|-------|
| 0 | Strange creature behavior, unusual sightings, uneasy atmosphere |
| 1 | Monsters stronger/organized, dark magic sightings, cultist activity |
| 1.5 | Seal cracks visibly. Betrayal timed to weakening. |
| 2 | Player underground. Surface deteriorates. |
| 3 | Gorath's armies emerging, sky darkens, temples losing power |
| 4 | Gorath nearly free. Final confrontation. |

---

## The Godsent Summoning

### The Rite of Calling
- Circle of Arrival (ancient stone platform in First City)
- Coalition of strongest mages and priests
- Immense magical energy (drains kingdom's reserves)
- King's authorization (desperate measure)
- Multiple parties summoned, not just the player

### Hidden Truth
A god nudged the ritual to ensure the player was summoned. Player doesn't learn this until late game, if at all.

---

## The Traitor Party

- A party of Godsents who defected to Gorath in secret for power
- **4 preset compositions** — one randomly selected per playthrough
- Each has unique leader, personality, combat style

### The Betrayal (Act 1.5)
1. King sends player + traitor party on joint mission
2. Traitors engineer player to go deeper
3. Ancient barrier trap activates — player locked inside
4. Traitors mock player through barrier, reveal deal with Gorath
5. They leave. Surface told player died heroically.
6. Only way out is DEEPER — into the Shadow World

---

## Companion System

- **Max party:** Player + 3 companions
- **Total companions:** 13
- **Any death (3rd injury) = GAME OVER**
- First companion: King offers 3 from guild, pick 1 or refuse
- Later companions: recruitment quests during missions
- Every companion has personal quest chain (success = upgrade, failure = stays same)
- All quests include companion_lines for party reactions

---

## The Shadow World (Act 2)

- Ancient underground realm, NOT Gorath's domain
- Own sky (bioluminescent caverns), ecosystems, civilizations

### Who Lives There
- **Fungal Deepkin** — connected to underground fungal networks
- **Crystal Deepkin** — made of/integrated with living crystal
- **Shadow Deepkin** — shadow-adapted humanoids, most common
- **Dark Mages & Exiles** — surface dwellers who fled underground
- **Ancient Dragons** — deepest caverns, not all hostile
- **Umbrath Worshippers** — hidden god's followers
- **Monsters** — shadow creatures, crystal beasts, cave horrors, ancient constructs

### Underground Cities (2-3)
- Dark mage enclave (merchants, forbidden knowledge)
- Ancient Deepkin city (alien culture, unique quests)
- Dangerous frontier settlement

## Key Lore Facts (Quick Reference)

1. Caelmund founded after Sealing War
2. Seal weakening — signs escalate through acts
3. Gorath wants to rule, not destroy
4. King's seer foresaw Gorath's avatar — triggered summoning
5. Summoning secretly rigged by unknown god
6. All Godsent groups meet in Act 0
7. 4 traitor party presets rotate per playthrough
8. Traitors found barrier trap themselves (no divine intel)
9. Shadow Hero is mysterious rogue Godsent — thread through entire game
10. Shadow World is independent of Gorath
11. Umbrath worshipped only in Shadow World
12. Deepkin: fungal, crystal, shadow subtypes
13. 13 companions total; first from King's guild, rest via recruitment
14. First companion conversation = quest + lore + relationship
15. Max 3 companions; any death = game over
16. Gods never appear directly
17. Act 3 is shadow war — covert, not loud revenge
18. Players choose from 3 missions unless required
