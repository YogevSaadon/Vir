## How to Add Entries

When writing a story that introduces:
- A named item with unique stats → add to Unique Items
- A named enemy beyond basic bestiary → add to Unique Enemies
- A named NPC → add to Unique NPCs

Companions and traitor party members are tracked in their own docs.
