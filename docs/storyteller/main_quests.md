# Vassnian Main Quest Table

Total: ~40 quests. ~19 per playthrough. 64 possible boss combinations (4x4x4).

**Boss paths per act:**
- Act 1: Goblin King / Bandit Warlord / Corrupted Beast / Cult High Priest
- Act 2: Shadow Dragon / Deepkin Tyrant / Exiled Archmage / Ancient Construct
- Act 3: Commander / Prodigy / Showman / Shepherd (traitor parties)

---

## Act 0 — Arrival

### 0.1 The Awakening (Universal)
Pulled through Circle of Arrival. King greets you. Other Godsents present. Assigned to King's Guild. Choose 1 of 3 companions or refuse all. *Tutorial, class already selected.*

### 0.2 Proving Ground (Universal)
Creatures near city walls. First real mission. Fight alongside companion. See other parties heading out. *First combat. Rank 1-2 enemies.*

### 0.3 Traitor Meeting (Boss-Specific)

**Commander — The War Table:** King summons all leaders for strategy briefing. Commander leads, pulls you aside after. "We should coordinate." Companion says he's a natural leader.

**Prodigy — Curious Minds:** Magical anomaly near city from mass summoning. Prodigy insists you investigate together. Solve puzzle as team. "You're not as slow as the others."

**Showman — Heroes' Welcome:** Tavern celebration organized by Showman. Free drinks, arm wrestling, stories. "To us! Ripped from our worlds and STILL having a better time than back home!"

**Shepherd — The Roadside Mercy:** Find Shepherd tending wounded travelers. Heals your companion's scratches unprompted. "It costs nothing to be kind."

## Act 1 — Growth

### 1.1 Boss Introduction (Boss-Specific)

**Goblin King — Raids at Dawn:** Coordinated goblin raids on eastern border. War paint, formations, siege ladders. Someone is uniting the tribes.

**Bandit Warlord — The Crimson Road:** Caravans vanishing. Ambushed by disciplined soldiers with crimson scarves. Deserter whispers about a Warlord with glowing artifact.

**Corrupted Beast — The Hunter's Mark:** Patrol destroyed in forest. Massive wrong creature tracking Godsents specifically. Too strong to engage — you flee.

**Cult Priest — Whispers in Stone:** Temple sites vandalized. Specific seal anchor points being weakened. Hidden ritual site with blood and dark symbols.

### 1.2 Boss Escalation + Traitor Bonding (Boss-Specific)

**Goblin King — The Goblin Court:** Massive goblin fortress. Thousands united under crowned king. Siege towers, war beasts, tunnels toward farmlands. Infiltrate and assess.

**Bandit Warlord — The Warlord's Artifact:** Track crimson bandits. The artifact changed a failed thief. Eyes glow, men feel pain resisting. Connected to Gorath's influence.

**Corrupted Beast — Run:** Beast finds you again, larger and more twisted. You CANNOT win. Desperate chase through hostile terrain. It's growing.

**Cult Priest — The Hidden Congregation:** Cult infiltrated multiple villages. Raid secret meeting, High Priest escapes. Captured cultists smile: "He stirs."

---

**Commander — Shield Brothers:** Joint dangerous mission. His tactics save your party. Campfire: "You think for yourself. That's rare." Offers handshake.

**Prodigy — The Forgotten Formula:** Help with dangerous research on Sealing War texts. Solve puzzle she couldn't crack alone. "You kept up with me." Shares real findings — seal IS breaking.

**Showman — Blood and Glory:** Hunt massive creature together. Exhilarating tracking and combat. Covered in monster blood, he grins: "THIS is what we were summoned for!"

**Shepherd — The Plague Village:** Village dying from strange sickness. You protect perimeter while she heals through the night. By dawn, children cling to her.

### 1.3 Boss Climax — BOSS FIGHTS

**Goblin King — The Goblin Throne:** Assault mountain fortress. Organized hordes, shield walls, war beasts. Goblin King on throne with iron crown. Multi-phase: guards then personal combat. Tactical fighter.

**Bandit Warlord — The Artifact's Price:** Storm canyon stronghold. Warlord with pulsing artifact. Corrupted power, dark blasts. When defeated: destroy artifact or claim it? (Story flag)

**Corrupted Beast — Stand and Fight:** Third encounter. You're stronger now. Corruption consumed it — shadow tendrils, wrong proportions. Brutal, primal fight. End its suffering.

**Cult Priest — The Seal Ritual:** Race to seal anchor. Cultists performing blood magic. High Priest channels Gorath's power directly. Timer mechanic: fail slowly = seal cracks further.

## Act 1.5 — Betrayal

**Commander — The Trap:** Joint mission. "You take the lower passage." Barrier activates. Through wall: "You were a variable I needed to remove. Nothing more." Walks away.

**Prodigy — The Trap:** Joint expedition to ancient ruin. "The resonance is strongest there." Barrier snaps shut. "I thought you'd figure it out sooner. I left hints."

**Showman — The Trap:** "One last adventure!" Barrier slams. Same smile, different eyes. "We had fun, didn't we? The friendship? Eh." Laughs walking away.

**Shepherd — The Trap:** Relief mission — trapped refugees. "People are dying." Barrier seals. "I'm sorry. I'm protecting them. Gorath isn't what you think."

## Act 2 — Shadow World

### 2.1 Introduction (Boss-Specific)

**Shadow Dragon — Dragon's Territory:** Vast cavern, bioluminescent crystals, dead stone forest. Everything burned. Locals whisper: "The dragon claims everything here."

**Deepkin Tyrant — The Sunless Court:** Deepkin soldiers capture you. Crystal city. Tyrant on black stone throne: "Serve the Court or be fed to the deep."

**Exiled Archmage — The Tower in the Dark:** Massive spire in cavern. Archmage fled surface decades ago. Constructs patrol. Voice from nowhere: "I'm the only law that matters here."

**Ancient Construct — The Builder's Grave:** Impossibly old ruins. Automated guardians. Massive construct blocks passage, scanning. Waiting for password? Key? Worthy inheritor?

### 2.2 Escalation

**Shadow Dragon — The Dragon's Bargain:** Dragon speaks. Offers deal: clear a rival predator, and it lets you pass. Choice: accept (morally grey) or find another way.

**Deepkin Tyrant — Rebellion Below:** Rebel faction contacts you. Want to overthrow Tyrant but have their own conquest agenda. Allies with strings.

**Exiled Archmage — The Mad Scholar:** Audience with Archmage. Incredible research but he's unraveling. Will share knowledge if you retrieve components. His experiment isn't safe.

**Ancient Construct — The Core:** Still-functioning power core. Three-way choice: repair (ally), override (seize power), destroy (eliminate threat). Echoes into Act 3-4.

### 2.3 Boss Fights

**Shadow Dragon — Dragonslayer:** Ancient wyrm in crystal cavern. Repositions through tunnels, targets weakest. Multi-phase: aerial strafing, ground combat, desperate final stand.

**Deepkin Tyrant — Overthrow:** Assault throne room. Elite guards, architectural traps. Tyrant with forgotten-age weapons. "I have ruled for centuries. You are a moment."

**Exiled Archmage — The Madman's Last Spell:** Tower warped by experiment. Reality bends, rooms shift. Each floor a different nightmare. At top: storm of raw magic. "I UNDERSTAND now!"

**Ancient Construct — The Awakened:** Building-sized, impossible materials. Every attack a calculated pattern that shifts when you adapt. Pattern-based boss. Possible non-lethal solution.

## Act 2.5 — Emergence (Universal)

**Return to the Light:** Passage to surface. Exhausting climb. Sunlight blinds you. But Caelmund has changed — corruption everywhere. In the capital, traitor party rules as heroes. Everyone thinks you're dead.

## Act 3 — Shadow War

### 3.1 Working from Shadows

**Commander — The General's Blindspot:** King's military right hand. Find officers who noticed inconsistencies. Build case while avoiding patrols.

**Prodigy — Unweaving the Web:** Controls magical defense network. KNOWS you're alive. Move through gaps, find blind spots. She adapts her detection web.

**Showman — The Legend's Lie:** Beloved folk hero. Songs in taverns, children imitate him. Must find witnesses against a man the kingdom loves.

**Shepherd — The Mercy Trap:** Runs refugee camps. Thousands depend on her. Must build alternative support before exposing her.

### 3.2 Escalation

**Commander — Turncoats:** Flip officers. Commander notices, tightens security. Trusted officer found dead. Clock ticking.

**Prodigy — Counterspell:** Sabotage ward network. She adapts faster than expected. "She's mapping YOUR magical signature." Need different approach.

**Showman — The Mask Cracks:** Gathered testimonies. He doubles down on charm. Behind doors, getting paranoid and violent. "He hit Lysette last night."

**Shepherd — Building Hope:** Parallel support network growing. Shepherd responds by increasing generosity. War of compassion.

### 3.3 Boss Fights

**Commander — Checkmate:** Corner him in war room. Same room where he earned your trust. Precise, tactical, commanding team like chess pieces. But you know his patterns now.

**Prodigy — Outsmarted:** Lure her where wards don't reach. For first time, she's surprised. Fights with everything — devastating spells, teleportation. Kill Tormund first (shield), track Shade, close on Yara.

**Showman — The Curtain Falls:** Expose publicly. Mask drops. "If I can't be loved, I'll be FEARED." Kill Old Harken FIRST (resurrection), disrupt Lysette's buffs, break Corin's aura.

**Shepherd — Shattered Halo:** Confront at main camp. Uses people as moral shield. When truth presented, some leave, some stay. Out-damage her healing or isolate from team.

---

## Act 4 — Gorath (Universal)

### 4.1 The Descent
Gorath's domain beneath kingdom. Cracked prison pulsing with dark energy. Environment hostile. Previous act allies may appear based on flags.

### 4.2 The Last Seal
Final seal anchor cracking. Gorath's voice: "You will kneel." Choices throughout game determine what you bring. Pre-boss gauntlet.

### 4.3 God of Conquest — FINAL BOSS
Gorath manifests. Multi-phase: avatar, then true form. Reality-warping attacks, corruption waves. Companion bonds affect ultimate abilities. Shadow World knowledge unlocks secret mechanics. Everything matters.
