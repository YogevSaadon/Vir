# Vassnian Traitor Parties (4 Presets)

One randomly selected per playthrough. Each: Godsent leader + 3 Caelmundi members.

---

## 1. The Iron Vanguard

**Leader: The Commander** (Male, Knight)
- **Combat:** Heavy armor, tower shield, formation tactics. Commands positioning. Buffs defense.
- **Personality:** Calm, strategic, measured. Never raises voice. Radiates competence.
- **Act 0:** Gives genuine tactical advice. Offers intel sharing. Mentor figure.
- **Act 1:** Joint mission where his leadership saves the day. Quiet moment about "protecting this world."
- **Betrayal:** Cold, calculated. "You were a variable I needed to remove. Nothing more."
- **Act 3:** King's military advisor. Generals answer to him. Politically untouchable.
- **Secret:** First Godsent Gorath contacted. Didn't hesitate — saw it as a promotion.

| Member | Role | Combat Note |
|--------|------|------------|
| Ser Aldric (M) | Paladin — off-tank, divine shields | Separate from Commander or he shields him |
| Miriel (F) | Priest — healer, dispels | May not fully know truth. Mercy option? |
| Halvard (M) | Warrior — greatsword, charges | Berserker rage: stronger as HP drops. Kill early. |

**Act 3 Challenge:** Political siege — evidence, allies, timing to dethrone a war hero.

---

## 2. The Shadow Circle

**Leader: The Prodigy** (Female, Mage)
- **Combat:** Glass cannon. Devastating AoE, teleportation, counterspells. Hardest to pin down.
- **Personality:** Brilliant, condescending but humorous. Makes you feel smart for keeping up.
- **Act 0:** Deciphers ancient text together. Seems excited for intellectual equals.
- **Act 1:** Discovers clue about seal weakening. Shares it openly (real info mixed with manipulation).
- **Betrayal:** Disappointed. "I thought you'd figure it out sooner. I left hints."
- **Act 3:** Runs magical defense network. Exposing her = kingdom loses magical protection.
- **Secret:** Figured out Gorath was real first. Instead of warning, saw opportunity to study god's power.

| Member | Role | Combat Note |
|--------|------|------------|
| Shade (M) | Rogue — assassin, stealth, poisons | Disappears mid-fight. Backstabs if you lose track. |
| Yara (F) | Ranger — sniper, elemental arrows | Extreme range. Close gap or she chips you down. |
| Tormund (M) | Mage — support, shields Prodigy | PRIORITY: While alive, Prodigy has magic shield. |

**Act 3 Challenge:** Magical countermeasures — outsmart someone always 3 steps ahead.

---

## 3. The Gilded Saints

**Leader: The Showman** (Male, Warrior)
- **Combat:** Dual-wield, counterattacks, taunts that debuff morale. Stronger with audience.
- **Personality:** Loud, funny, magnetic. Makes you feel like his best friend in 5 minutes.
- **Act 0:** Arm around player at tavern. Toasts "to us Godsents!" Makes everyone laugh.
- **Act 1:** Saves village together. Lets you take credit publicly while his people spread his stories.
- **Betrayal:** Grinning. "Oh come on, don't look so hurt! The friendship? Eh..." Laughs while fighting.
- **Act 3:** Folk hero. Songs in every tavern. Feeds poor, puts on perfect show. Behind doors: ruthless.
- **Secret:** Nobody in original world. Gorath offered what he always wanted: to be loved and powerful.

| Member | Role | Combat Note |
|--------|------|------------|
| Sister Corin (F) | Paladin — tank, holy smite, morale aura | Morale aura buffs whole party. Silence/debuff first. |
| Old Harken (M) | Priest — healer, resurrection, holy fire | MUST kill first or he resurrects fallen. Top priority. |
| Lysette (F) | Bard — buffs, debuffs, charm | Songs stack buffs over time. Rush or disrupt. |

**Act 3 Challenge:** Hearts and minds — shatter the legend without becoming the villain.

---

## 4. The Hollow Mercy

**Leader: The Shepherd** (Female, Paladin/Healer)
- **Combat:** Heavy armor, healing aura, divine barriers. Very hard to kill. Outlasts everyone.
- **Personality:** Warm, gentle, remembers names, brings medicine. Kindness seems completely real.
- **Act 0:** Heals your companion unprompted. Shares supplies. Asks about your world.
- **Act 1:** Runs field hospital. Priest companion: "what we should all aspire to be."
- **Betrayal:** Sad. "I'm sorry. I'm protecting them — all of them. Gorath isn't what you think."
- **Act 3:** Runs refugee camps. Thousands depend on her. Taking her down collapses support unless you build alternative first.
- **Secret:** Burned out caretaker in original world. Gorath showed her a world where she'd never be forgotten.

| Member | Role | Combat Note |
|--------|------|------------|
| Dorek (M) | Warrior — tower shield, intercepts attacks | Bodyguard: physically blocks attacks on Shepherd. |
| Nessa (F) | Mage — nature magic, vines, terrain control | Area denial. Locks movement while Shepherd heals. |
| Brother Fenn (F) | Priest — dark priest hiding as light, curses | Stacking debuffs. Every turn undealt, party weakens. |

**Act 3 Challenge:** Unmasking the saint — she IS hope for thousands. Exposing her could destroy more than it saves.

## Summary
- 2 Male leaders (Commander, Showman) / 2 Female (Prodigy, Shepherd)
- Each traitor gets: Act 0 meeting + Act 1 bonding + Act 1.5 betrayal + Act 3 boss arc
- One party randomly selected per run
- 4 x 4 x 4 = 64 possible boss combinations across all acts
