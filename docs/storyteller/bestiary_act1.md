# Vassnian Bestiary — Act 1

53 enemy types across 19 families and 5 ranks. Stats are tier-based (0-6).

**Encounter Power = (Sum of all enemy ranks) / 4** (always divide by 4)

---

## Tier System
| Tier | Label |
|------|-------|
| 0 | None |
| 1 | Minimal |
| 2 | Low |
| 3 | Medium |
| 4 | High |
| 5 | Max |
| 6+ | Over Max (must have low stats elsewhere) |

## Act 1 Distribution
- **1.1 (Lv1-2):** Rank 1-2 — Slimes, Rats, Wolves, Bats, Insects, Bandits, Goblins
- **1.2 (Lv2-3):** Rank 2-3 — Upgraded Goblins/Bandits, Skeletons, Undead, Elementals, Harpies
- **1.3 (Lv4-5):** Rank 3-5 — Cultists, Dire Beasts, Ogres, Trolls, Corrupted, Dark Knights

Boss-themed enemies layer ON TOP during sub-act events.
